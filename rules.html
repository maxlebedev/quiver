Quiver Rules

Character Creation

  • Name, pronouns
  • Select two backgrounds
    • Each is a Description + Occupation pair. One is your past, one is your present. Tell us about them
  • Pick items
    • Any 1 item from the Signature Item list
    • Any 5 items from the Starting Item list
  • Each player picks a sentence from the bonds table, and another player
    • Agree on how the bond applies to your relationship
    • Repeat until every player has at least two bonds
  • Work with the other players to come up with a group identity

It's best to bring enough Torches, Rations, Weapons, and Armor. Take a look at the Kits for inspiration

Game Terms

Bane: Remove a d6 from your dice pool for each bane

Background: A phrase representing who you are or used to be. +d6 to relevant rolls

Crit: Attack that rolls max die value.

Hit: Attack is greater than enemy Armor.

Graze: Attack at or under enemy Armor.

Bulky: Takes 2 inventory slots

Tiny: Takes half of an inventory slot

Treatable: A wound that can be temporarily alleviated by a Poultice

Stacks X: X of these fit per inventory slot

Step Up: Increase the die size one step d4➺d6➺d8➺d10➺d12

Step Down: Decrease the die size one step d12➺d10➺d8➺d6➺d4

Push: Pay a cost, then reroll any rolled dice

Guard: Your buffer against harm. Losing guard represents giving ground, narrowly avoiding danger, minor scrapes

Usage Dice

When there is a question of how long something lasts, such as a torch or ongoing spell.

Roll 6d6. If the highest result is a 5 or lower, the effect ends

Each time you roll again for that effect, do so with one more bane

Inventory

Your inventory is the center of your character sheet. It stores your items, as well as any conditions you have. You have 12 inventory slots. 2 are your hands, 10 stowed away.

Conditions

If you get a condition but have no free slots, break an item and place the condition over it.

  • Fatigue: gained by failing to cast spells, skipping food/sleep, pushing rolls
  • Wound: gained by taking damage. A shattered knee. A missing eye. A shadow curse

Relic Magic

Items that hold magic power are called Relics. To invoke the relic's magic, spend a charge. Relics regain their charges when resting in a place of power associated with the relic's origin

Alchemy

Using Alchemical Supplies, you may harvest magical plants and creatures into vials (once per source). While in Camp, you may turn those reagents into potions. The potions temporarily give the drinker the reagent's properties.

At character creation: pick 3 potion effects, GM picks side-effects

Rest

True recovery happens only during an extended stay in a place of civilization and safety. Barring that, it is always possible to set up camp

Camping takes a quarter day. During camp, players may:

  • Mend Armor with a Tinker's Kit
  • Make Rations out of plants and animals with Cooking Gear
  • Make Potions with Alchemical Supplies and reagents
  • Scout ahead, learning about nearby areas
  • Keep watch, gain the upper hand in any camp encounter
  • Make a Poultice (Action roll)

Eating a ration clears one Fatigue. Eating a second clears another. Not eating gains one Fatigue

Action Rolls

Whenever you use your skill to attempt an uncertain action, build and roll a dice pool

  • Take one die for attempting the action
  • Take one die if your inventory isn't full
  • Take one die if you have a relevant background
  • Take one die if you have relevant equipment
  • Take one die if you have assistance
  • Remove one die for every bane

If the pool has 0 dice, roll two dice and take the lower

If the highest die you rolled was a 6, you succeed

You may gain Fatigue to push

Reaction Rolls

When you avoid a danger or cast a spell, build and roll a dice pool

  • Start with 6 dice
  • Take a bane for every condition you have
  • Take a bane if your inventory is full

If the pool has 0 dice, roll two dice and take the lower

If the highest die you rolled was a 6, you succeed

You may gain Fatigue to push

Words of Power

A Grimoire contains Words of Power that can be used to cast spells

To cast a spell, hold the Grimoire in both hands and construct a two-Word phrase. Explain the intent of your spell

Make a Reaction roll

  • Spells that stretch their Words, cause direct harm, or require immense power take banes
  • On a success, the spell is cast
    • If the spell deals damage, roll a d6 for each bane
  • On a failure, the caster gains one Fatigue
  • The caster may gain another Fatigue to push

Combat

Initiative

  • Pass a Reaction roll or skip the first turn of combat
  • Enemies always act after players
  • At the end of your turn, choose an ally to act next

Attack Procedure

Roll your weapon's Attack die and compare it to enemy Armor.

If you roll over their Armor, you Hit. Otherwise, you Graze. If you roll the highest die result, you Crit instead.

CritDeal your Attack as direct damage (ignoring Armor) and reduce enemy Armor by 1
HitDeal the difference in damage or reduce Armor by 1
GrazeTake the difference as retaliation damage. Enemy chooses to take the full attack or reduce their Armor by 1

Guard

  • Players start every combat with 6 Guard, which Enemies deplete by attacking
  • When your Guard hits 0, roll on the Wound Table
  • Then reset your Guard to max and apply carryover damage

Maneuvers

  • You may attempt one Maneuver in addition to anything you do on your turn
  • Declare your intent
  • Make an Action roll with banes representing its difficulty
  • Regardless of outcome, an enemy makes an attack against you

Inventory items

  • Interacting with items that aren't in your hands is a Maneuver

Wounds

When you gain a Wound, roll d6. Add 1 for each old wound

2Superficial disfigurement7Decrease maximum Guard by 2. Treatable
3Destroy a random inventory item8Lose a limb
4Bane to Action rolls. Treatable9Lose consciousness. Reach town within 1 day or die
